What makes DiaWise different

Not just another AI chatbot

Every feature is designed around one purpose: helping people understand and manage diabetes with confidence and clarity.

📚

Evidence-grounded answers

Responses draw from curated diabetes medical literature. Every answer shows the exact source document and page. No mystery black box.

🎯

Two intelligent modes

Switch between Learning mode for rich educational depth, and Newly Diagnosed mode for warmer, simpler, reassuring answers at an emotional moment.

🔬

Transparent source panel

A live evidence panel sits beside every conversation, showing sources, page numbers, and whether the answer came from the knowledge base or general knowledge.

🧠

Persistent memory

Your full chat history is saved locally in your browser. Come back a week later and pick up exactly where you left off. No account, no sign-up, no data uploaded.

Smart follow-up engine

After every answer, DiaWise suggests 2-3 intelligent follow-up questions generated from the context, so your learning deepens naturally without dead ends.

🛡️

Safety-first by design

Emergency keywords trigger immediate escalation to 999/911 before any LLM call. Every answer carries a clear educational disclaimer. Hallucinated citations are structurally prevented.
